## About

I study Computer Engineering at the University of California, San Diego,
advised by [Prof. Hadi Esmaeilzadeh](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~hadi/). My
research interests center on computer architecture, with a focus on
acceleration for emerging workloads.
