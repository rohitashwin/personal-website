About Me

I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering at the University of California, San Diego, advised by Prof. Hadi Esmaeilzadeh. My research interests center on computer architecture, with a particular focus on designing custom hardware accelerators for robotics and secure LLM inference, as well as repurposing old hardware to support sustainable, green computing.
